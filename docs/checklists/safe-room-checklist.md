# Safe-Room Checklist

Printable. Work top to bottom before pickup day. Pair with
[chapter 2](../02-the-safe-room.md).

## The room

- [ ] Low-traffic room chosen (not the hallway bathroom everyone uses)
- [ ] Door closes fully and *stays closed* (household knows the rule)
- [ ] Windows closed and latched; window screens intact
- [ ] Quiet heating/cooling; no direct draft on the bed spot
- [ ] Lights can be left soft/dim

## Hazards — remove or secure

- [ ] Toxic plants removed (lilies — lethal to cats — pothos, philodendron,
      sago palm, and anything else you haven't verified)
- [ ] No dangling blind cords or curtain ties at pet height
- [ ] Electrical cords tucked or covered (kitten/chew territory)
- [ ] Trash can has a lid or has left the room
- [ ] Small ingestibles cleared: coins, hair ties, buttons, pills, rubber
      bands, kids' toys
- [ ] Itchy breakables removed from reachable surfaces
- [ ] *(Dogs)* Gum and candy out of reach — xylitol is extremely dangerous
- [ ] *(Cats)* No open water sources: toilet lids down, no open buckets

## Stations — spread around the room

- [ ] Food bowl placed, away from water, away from litter
- [ ] Water bowl, filled fresh
- [ ] *(Cats)* Litter box in a quiet corner — **one box per cat plus one,
      minimum for the household; scoop daily**
- [ ] Bed or soft blanket where they can sleep unseen but feel connected
- [ ] Scratcher for cats; chew-safe item for dogs

## Hides and comfort

- [ ] At least two hiding spots (box on its side, covered bed, draped chair)
- [ ] One item with the rescue/shelter's scent (their old blanket or towel)
- [ ] One item with *your* scent (an worn t-shirt, placed near the bed)
- [ ] Carrier available in the room, door removed or wedged open — a free
      carrier becomes a den

## Supplies in place

- [ ] The exact food they've been eating (ask the rescue the brand + formula)
- [ ] Treats for reward throwing (distance, not hand-feeding yet)
- [ ] Enzymatic cleaner for accidents
- [ ] Leash + collar/harness ready (dogs); carrier usable (cats)
- [ ] Vet's number **and** the nearest 24-hour emergency clinic saved in your
      phone

## Day-one behavior plan

- [ ] Everyone in the house knows: go in calmly, sit, ignore, don't reach
- [ ] Children briefed: flat hand only, never wake a sleeping pet, no chasing
- [ ] Existing pets kept out this week (see [chapter 7](../07-introducing-family-and-pets.md))
- [ ] The door stays closed until *the pet* asks about the world
- [ ] [Log sheet](first-week-log-sheet.md) printed and on the
      fridge

---
*Education only — never veterinary advice. When in doubt, call your vet.*
